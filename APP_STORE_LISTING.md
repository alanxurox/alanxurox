**Living alone? Still Here is your safety net.**

Check in daily with one button tap. If you miss check-ins for 2 consecutive days, we automatically email your emergency contacts to make sure you're okay.

**WHY STILL HERE?**

**HOW IT WORKS:**

1. Add 1-3 emergency contacts (family, friends, neighbors)
2. Check in daily by tapping the button
3. If you miss 2 days, we send them an email alert
4. That's it. We told you it was simple.

**PERFECT FOR:**

• Young professionals living alone in cities
• Digital nomads and solo travelers
• Remote workers away from family
• International students far from home
• Neurodivergent adults who forget to check in
• Elderly parents maintaining independence
• Anyone who's ever worried "what if something happens?"

**FEATURES:**

✓ One-tap daily check-in
✓ Customizable reminder time
✓ Adjustable alert threshold (1-7 days)
✓ Streak counter (gamified motivation)
✓ Vacation mode (pause alerts while traveling)
✓ Personal message for emergency contacts

Still Here is NOT a replacement for medical alert services. It does not:
• Monitor your health or detect falls
• Track your location via GPS
• Contact emergency services (911/999)

Still Here is a supplemental check-in tool. Always call emergency services in true emergencies.

Life Alert felt too "senior." Free apps sold my data. Enterprise solutions were overkill.

Then I saw China's viral "Are You Dead Yet?" app. Brilliant concept. So I built the Western version.

One month of development. Zero venture capital. Just a simple solution to a real problem.

• No advertisements ever
• Minimal data collection (email, check-ins only)
• Full data export and deletion on request

### FAQ Section

**Q: Is this a medical alert system?**
A: No. Still Here does NOT replace medical alert services. It doesn't detect falls, track health, or call 911. It's a simple check-in system to notify loved ones if you go silent. Always call emergency services in real emergencies.

**Q: What if I forget to check in?**
A: We send a reminder at your chosen time (default 9 AM). If you still don't check in for 2 days, we email your emergency contacts. They can reach out to make sure you're okay.

**Q: Can I pause it when I travel?**
A: Yes! Vacation Mode pauses all check-ins and alerts. Enable it with one tap.

**Q: Do you track my location?**
A: Nope. We don't use GPS, we don't track your movements, and we don't know where you are. Ever.

**Q: What data do you collect?**
A: Only what's necessary: your email (for account), emergency contact emails (to alert them), and check-in timestamps (to know if you missed one). That's it. We don't sell data. We don't have ads. We work for you.

**Q: What happens if someone actually dies?**
A: Our app sends an email to emergency contacts if check-ins are missed. What happens next depends on them. Still Here is a tool, not a guarantee. It's supplemental safety, not a replacement for proper emergency planning.

**Q: Why not just use a free app?**
A: Free apps either (a) sell your data, (b) bombard you with ads, or (c) are actually $X/month subscriptions in disguise. We charge $7.99 once so we never have to do any of that. Our incentives align with yours.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Yes. Within 14 days, full refund, no questions asked. Email support@stillhereapp.com.

**Q: What if I change phones?**
A: Your account works across devices. Sign in on your new phone and everything's there.

**Q: Is this available internationally?**
A: Currently iOS and Android in US, UK, Canada, Australia. More countries coming soon!
